whatsnew 1
; What's new, shown once after the menu file is updated (whats_new.c) and
; from Settings > Console > About > What's New. Our own words.
;
;   version <x.y>   starts a version's list (newest first in the file)
;   item <name>     a row (about 30 characters at most)
;   go <path>       optional: where A "Show me" goes, as Settings shows it:
;                   "Look > Theme > Animations" (heading > page > row; a row's
;                   own page adds one more: "Look > Assistant > Fairy Look... > Fairy Color")
;   about <text>    what Vee says about it (about 200 characters at most)
;
; Paths must match settings_menu.c (tests/whats_new_check.py checks them).

version 0.5
item Status marks
go Games > Library > Game Status
about Mark a game Playing, Beaten, 100% or Backlog: Z on its cover, then Status. C-up / C-down step to each, and Backlog and Beaten turn up on the Collections tab.
item Library Stats
go Games > Saves & Play > Library Stats
about Your games, their size, your time played, your five most played, top developers and genres, and the SD card's free space, all on one page.
item Year in Review
go Games > Saves & Play > Library Stats
about On Library Stats, C-right steps to a page per year: time played, games, most played and the games you beat, against the year before.
item Settings search
about Press Z on any page of Settings and type a word or two. Every setting with them is listed with where it lives, and A takes you straight there.
item Setup wizard
go Console > About > Setup Wizard
about I can walk you through the main choices again any time: your TV, the picture, regions and names, the look and how I talk.
item Meet Vee
go Look > Assistant > Assistant
about That's me! I pop up now and then with a tip, welcome you back from your games and have a word about the ones you rest on. Rarely, Chatty or off: you choose.
item Ask me a question
go Look > Assistant > Ask a Question
about Answers about the whole menu, in eight topics. Also last in the Z menu on the covers.
item Make me your own
go Look > Assistant > Fairy Look...
about Give me a new name, another colour, a new way to fly in and move about, and a glow that breathes.
item Showy animations
go Look > Theme > Animations
about Covers glide and pop, pop-ups grow open, Settings slides between pages, and the cover flies into Game info. Snappy keeps it all quick and plain.
item The box opens
go Look > Start-up > Game Start
about Starting a game opens its box: the cartridge pops out, spins while the game loads and snaps into place.
item Covers deal in
about At start-up the cover row deals itself in, and back from a game its cartridge rises spinning into its cover. Both follow Cover Slide.
item Clips on the covers
go Look > Game Info > Clips on Covers
about With the Logos-and-Clips pack and the Expansion Pak, the selected game's clip plays in its cover's place, even on the 3D box, and carries on into Game info.
item The versions column
go Look > Covers > Versions Column
about A cover standing for several files opens them as a column of covers over a blurred Games screen, with when each was last played.
item Time Played, Never Played
go Games > Library > Sort By
about Sort By Time Played puts your most played first, and C-down on the Games tab reaches the games you've never started.
item Collection progress
go Look > Badges > Collection Progress
about Each collection's card says how many of its games you've beaten, with a bar, and Complete once you've beaten them all.
item Test patterns
go Console > Tools > Test Patterns
about Borders at 2.5, 5 and 10 percent to see what your TV cuts off, and color bars with grey steps to set its brightness and color.
item Controller test
go Console > Tools > Controller Test
about Every button lights up in its place, the stick's reach is measured with a wear rating, and Z + A tries a Rumble Pak.
item A new Settings
about Three headings on L and R (Look, Games, Console) with short pages, icons, and a bar beside every setting you've changed.
item Quicker typing
go Console > Controls > Name Entry
about On the keyboard, holding B keeps erasing and Z closes it without keeping the name.
