## What the background music's licence asks for

- The wording: `Music by Eric Matyas www.soundimage.org`
- It must appear in the product itself (for us, the menu's credits screen),
  not only in a README or on a download page.
- The music may not be used in obscene or pornographic media.
- A licence without the credit costs $30 per track (ematyas@aol.com).

The file was converted from the site's download to WAV (stereo, 22,050 Hz,
8-bit), which the licence allows.
