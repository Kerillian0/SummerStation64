# Picture credits

Every picture in this folder is drawn by the two scripts here
(`make_icons.py`, `make_wordmark.py`); none is a photo or anyone else's
artwork. Two outside things go into them:

| What | Used for | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `wordmark/Anton-Regular.ttf` | the letters of "SUMMERSTATION" in `wordmark.png` | The Anton Project Authors | SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`wordmark/Anton-OFL.txt`) |
| `wordmark/MrDafoe-Regular.ttf` | the "64" in `wordmark.png` | Alejandro Paul (Sudtipos) | SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`wordmark/MrDafoe-OFL.txt`) |

The Open Font License allows using the fonts in pictures and passing the
font files on with their licence text, which is why both sit here.

Reference pictures the project owner supplied were used for style only
(an 80s title treatment for the wordmark; a recolored N64 logo for the
intro's spinning logo and its two patterns). Nothing was copied from them.
The spinning logo's shape is Nintendo's N64 logo; see `CLAUDE.md`.
