# SummerStation64 v0.5a

A small update to v0.5: one crash fixed, plus two new idle options.

## 🔧 Fixed

- **"height must be positive" crash without the Expansion Pak.** Pressing B
  on Game info while the box's top or bottom picture was showing could stop
  the menu (with Animations on Showy). Thanks to fmj777 for the report and photo.
- **Start Folder help** in Settings now points to the right place: open the
  Folders tab, go into the folder, press Z and choose "Set current directory
  as default".

## ✨ New

Both are in **Settings > Console > TV & Video** and are **off** unless you
switch them on.

- **Attract Mode:** leave a row of covers alone for 2, 5 or 10 minutes and
  the covers drift past on their own, each game's clip playing as it goes by
  (clips need the Expansion Pak). Choose Right to Left or Left to Right. The
  first button press stops it.
- **Dim Screen:** leave the menu alone for 5, 10 or 20 minutes, on any screen,
  and the picture dims and the music goes quieter. The first button press
  wakes it.

## 📥 Updating from v0.5

**Only `sc64menu.n64` changed.** Copy it over the old one in the top folder of
your SD card. Your Covers, Logos and Clips from v0.5 stay as they are (the box
art file is the same).

New to SummerStation64? Copy `sc64menu.n64` to the top folder of your SD card
and unzip `SummerStation64-v0.5a-Covers-Logos-and-Clips.zip` there too. The
[Companion Guide](https://kerillian0.github.io/SummerStation64/companion/#guide-setup)
walks through the rest.
